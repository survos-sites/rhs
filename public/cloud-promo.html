The Problem

  • Millions of irreplaceable documents — photographs, deeds, letters, oral histories — are deteriorating in boxes in basements and closets.
  • Commercial digitization services are expensive. Most small museums can't afford them.
  • Staff with technical skills are rare. Volunteers who know the collection are aging out.
  • Grants exist, but digitization isn't just buying a scanner — it's a whole system. Outside firms charge too much for a shoebox of donated photos. We help you do it yourself.
  • Even when collections are digitized, they're often hidden — no searchable website, no discoverability.

How ScanStationAI Works

Two parts: a simple scanner at your site, powerful AI in the cloud.

📷 The Scanner

High-resolution overhead camera. Place your document, press a pedal, done. Non-destructive — nothing gets pressed flat. Handles photos, books, letters, maps.

🔌 Simple Setup

Scanner and processing box connect to power and your network. We will come to you to demo, train, and discuss grant possibilities.

🧠 AI Assists, Humans Add Context

Our cloud system reads handwriting, suggests titles and keywords. But only your staff knows who donated it, what the photograph shows, the story behind it. They add that context — and can even voice-record notes while scanning.

🔍 Discoverable Results

Your collection becomes searchable — by keyword, by meaning, by date. Export in standard formats. Publish to your website.

🚗 We Come to You

We're based in Sperryville, VA. We drive to your location for installation and training. No shipping equipment back and forth.

💰 Grant Funding Covers It

Grants can pay for professional scanning help. We also help draft the technical sections of your grant application.

What the Operator Does

Human expertise + AI assistance. The operator knows the collection; the system helps organize it.

1

Organize

Decide the workflow — what types of items go where, how they should be tagged. You know your collection best.

2

Scan

Place item on the mat, press the pedal. That's it. Fast, consistent, anyone can do it.

3

Review & Enrich

AI suggests titles and keywords. You add the context the AI couldn't know — who donated it, what's in the photo, the story behind it.

4

Share

Export in standard formats. Publish. Your collection is now discoverable by researchers, genealogists, and the public.

Ways to Partner

Three options, from least commitment to most.

Option 1: Pilot Site (No Cost to You)

We're applying for an IMLS National Leadership Grant. Your institution could be named as a pilot site — all we need is a one-paragraph letter of support.

We'll digitize a portion of your collection as a proof of concept, working out the kinks in our system. This gives you a working demo you can use to apply for your own grant to finish the job.

Your cost: A letter of support

Option 2: Virginia Humanities Grant

You apply for a Virginia Humanities Regular Grant ($3,500–$15,000). Museado Foundation is the paid contractor providing technology and support.

Grants can fund professional scanning help. Volunteer hours and facility use cover the cost share — no cash required from you.

LOI due March 13 • Full app due April 17

Option 3: IMLS Inspire! Grant

Federal IMLS Inspire! Grant up to $75,000 specifically for small museums. Funds technology, digitization, and capacity building.

Requests under $25,000 require no cost share at all. This could fund real change — paid scanning help and meaningful operational growth.

Due March 13 • Up to $75,000

We can handle most of the paperwork. No pressure — just want to make sure you know all your options.
